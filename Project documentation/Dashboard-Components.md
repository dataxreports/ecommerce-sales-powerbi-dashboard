## 6. Dashboard Components

| Dashboard Component | Chart Type | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Total Sales | KPI Card | Displays total sales generated across the selected data. |
| Total Orders | KPI Card | Shows the number of unique orders. |
| Average Order Value | KPI Card | Displays average sales value per order. |
| Average Shipping Days | KPI Card | Shows the average number of days between order date and ship date. |
| Sales by Year | Column Chart | Compares annual sales performance from 2015 to 2018. |
| Monthly Sales Trend | Line Chart | Visualizes monthly sales trends over time. |
| Sales by Category | Bar Chart | Compares sales across Technology, Furniture, and Office Supplies. |
| Sales by Sub-Category | Bar Chart | Identifies sub-categories with higher sales. |
| Sales by Region | Bar Chart | Compares sales performance across regions. |
| Sales by Ship Mode | Bar Chart | Compares total sales across shipping methods. |
| Average Shipping Days by Ship Mode | Bar Chart | Compares average shipping duration across shipping methods. |
| Top 10 States by Sales | Bar Chart | Displays the ten states with the highest sales. |
